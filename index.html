Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

AttendMark is a classroom attendance system built by Manoj Choudhary as an academic project for FoCP (CSE-1003). This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what data the system stores and who can see it.

What data is collected

  • Teacher accounts: full name and email address, created through Firebase Authentication. Passwords are handled entirely by Firebase — they are never stored on this page and are not visible to the builder or to any admin.
  • Attendance records: when a student marks attendance, the system stores the student's name, scholar number, the session and subject, the time of marking, and how it was marked (QR/code or added manually by the teacher). If the teacher has enabled the location check for that session, the record also stores the student's location and its distance from the classroom location, used only to verify the student was in the classroom area.
  • Sessions: subject, section/class, room, session codes, and the teacher's email address used to send the register. When the location check is on, the session also stores the classroom location captured from the teacher's device at the start of the session.

How the data is used

Data is used only to run attendance: showing the teacher a live register, keeping a history of past sessions, producing the CSV register and the email-ready register, and letting authorised admins oversee the system. Nothing else.

Who can see the data

  • A teacher can see their own sessions and their own attendance records.
  • Authorised admin accounts can see all teachers, sessions and attendance records, for oversight and support.
  • Students do not have accounts and cannot browse anyone's data.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). In Demo Mode, nothing leaves your device — demo data exists only in the open browser tab and is erased when the tab is closed.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no analytics trackers, and no selling or sharing of data with third parties.
  • No data is collected from students beyond the name and scholar number they type into the marking form — plus, only when a session's location check is on, the location check described above. Location is never tracked in the background or outside the moment of marking.

Your choices

Teachers and students can ask for their records to be corrected or deleted at any time. Send the request to hello@manojchoudhary.me with the email address or scholar number involved, and it will be actioned from the Firebase console.

Terms of Use

Last updated: 20 November 2026

By using AttendMark you agree to these terms. AttendMark is an academic project built for FoCP (CSE-1003) and is provided free of charge, as-is, for classroom attendance.

Accounts

  • Teachers are responsible for their own account and password, and for the accuracy of the sessions they create.
  • Admin access is restricted to authorised accounts appointed by the builder. Do not attempt to access the Admin panel or another teacher's data without authorisation.

Marking attendance

  • Students may mark attendance only for themselves, and only while physically present in the classroom during a live session.
  • Marking attendance for another student (proxy marking), sharing the rotating code outside the classroom, or marking from outside the class is prohibited. The rotating code, the one-entry-per-scholar-number rule and the “Already marked” block exist to prevent this, and a teacher may treat a blocked or false entry as absent.
  • If a student has no phone or no connectivity, the teacher may add them manually; the teacher is responsible for the accuracy of manual entries.
  • If a session has the location check on, a student must allow location access and be within 150 metres of the classroom location to mark present; a mark attempted from outside that area is rejected.

Honest limitations

The rotating code and duplicate checks make proxy marking very difficult, but no attendance system is 100% proxy-proof. AttendMark records should be read alongside the teacher's own judgement. The service may occasionally be unavailable (for example, Firebase or network issues), and attendance taken during an outage should be reconciled by the teacher.

Acceptable use

  • Use the system only for genuine classroom attendance.
  • Do not enter false names or scholar numbers, attempt to disrupt sessions, or try to bypass the security checks.

Changes and availability

As a student project, AttendMark may be updated, paused or discontinued at any time. These terms and the Privacy Policy may be revised; the current version will always be the one shown on this page.
